Wei-Lin Chen, PhD

陳威霖

Associate Professor, Center for Teacher Education & Institute of Education
National Sun Yat-sen University, Kaohsiung, Taiwan

I study how social inequality shapes education and health across the transition to adulthood — from adverse childhood experiences and family dynamics to college pathways, sleep, and mental health — in the United States, Taiwan, and other Asian societies.

Research

Two connected programs

A
United States & Comparative

Education & Health Inequality among Youth

How adverse childhood experiences, school and college environments, and fields of study shape emerging adults' education, employment, sleep, substance use, and mental health — including STEM persistence among disadvantaged students and cross-national comparisons using PISA.

  • Adverse childhood experiences
  • College & STEM pathways
  • Sleep & health
  • Cross-national
B
Asian Societies

Inequality in Education & Health

Family processes, educational pathways, and well-being in Taiwan and South Korea; Indigenous students' experiences of racial microaggressions; and determinants of Indigenous health and health disparities in Taiwan.

  • Indigenous students
  • Microaggressions
